## YYYY-MM-DD - Avoid Trigonometric Functions in GLSL Hash Functions
**Learning:** Pure ALU-based hash functions (like Dave Hoskins' method without the added `tan`/`atan`) are much more performant than those utilizing trigonometric functions like `cos`, `tan`, or `atan`. Trigonometric operations heavily tax the Special Function Units (SFU) in the GPU, creating bottlenecks especially when used heavily in loops like the 3D noise generation (`noised`) or fractal brownian motion (`fbm3D`) within this path-tracer.
**Action:** Always prefer ALU-based pseudo-random number generators (like `hash11`, `hash12`, etc. based on fractional multiplication and dots) over trigonometric methods. Do not insert trig functions back into ALU hash methods, and alias generic hash wrappers to ALU versions to avoid SFU pipeline stalls.
