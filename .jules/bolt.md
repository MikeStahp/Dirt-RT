## 2024-05-24 - SFU Bottlenecks in Hash Algorithms
**Learning:** In this Vulkanite path tracing shader, the `hash` functions (`hash12`, `hash13`, `hash14`) were relying on expensive trigonometric operations (`tan`, `atan`) combined with `dot` and `fract`. In GLSL/GPU architectures, transcendental and trigonometric functions heavily rely on Special Function Units (SFUs) inside SMs/RT cores. Heavy reliance on these in loops or frequent calls like random number generation/noise mapping can stall the standard ALUs due to low SFU throughput. Dave Hoskins' pure ALU method (using `fract`, `dot` with prime multipliers and constant offsets) accomplishes uniform distribution with much higher ALU efficiency.
**Action:** Replace `tan(dot(x)*20*atan(x))` styled pseudo-random hash functions with Hoskins' pure ALU `dot(x, x.yzw + C)` approach in all hash/noise generation routines to bypass SFU bottlenecks entirely.
